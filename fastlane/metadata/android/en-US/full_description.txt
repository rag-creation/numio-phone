Phone by Numio is a private phone app for Android, part of the Numio app family.

<ul>
<li>No internet permission: the app never goes online</li>
<li>No ads, analytics, tracking or accounts</li>
<li>Everything the app keeps stays on your phone</li>
<li>Quiet Numio design with dark, light and system themes</li>
</ul>

Calls themselves still go through Android and your carrier, as with any phone app.

Free and open source under the GPL-3.0.
